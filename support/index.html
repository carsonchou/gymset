GymSet AI 支援

聯絡我們

使用上遇到問題、想回報錯誤，或有任何疑問，請寄信到 support@gymsetai.com。來信時請附上你註冊帳號的 Email 和問題說明。

檢舉不當內容

如果你在對戰中看到不當的照片或暱稱，可以在對戰畫面對手卡片右下角點「檢舉或封鎖」直接檢舉，或寄信到 support@gymsetai.com，並附上你的帳號 Email、對手的暱稱、對戰日期，以及內容說明。我們收到檢舉後，會在 24 小時內檢視；確認違規後，我們會移除相關內容，並可以停用違規帳號。你也可以在同一個選單封鎖對方。對戰中的「否決」按鈕只用來判定當天打卡不算數，不是檢舉機制。詳見使用條款。

GymSet AI Support

Contact us

If something isn't working, you want to report a bug, or you have a question, email support@gymsetai.com. Please include the email address of your account and a description of the problem.

Report inappropriate content

If you see an inappropriate photo or nickname in a battle, tap "Report or block" at the bottom right of your opponent's card on the battle screen, or email support@gymsetai.com. Include your account email, your opponent's nickname, the battle date, and a description of the problem. We review reports within 24 hours of receiving them. If we confirm a violation, we will remove the content and may disable the account. You can also block the user from the same menu. The "Reject" button in a battle only marks that day's check-in as not counting. It is not a way to report content. See the Terms of Use.
